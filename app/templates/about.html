Meet the Team

Our Story

Duncan Family Dentistry has been proudly serving Eastern Kentucky & Southern West Virginia communities for more than 25 years. Founded by Dr. Tiffany Duncan DMD, our mission is simple: deliver state-of-the-art dental care with true hometown warmth.

We invest in the latest technology—from Digital Panoramic X-rays to Rotary Endodontics machine; so every visit is efficient, comfortable, and precise. Above all, we treat each patient like family, celebrating milestones and brightening smiles across generations.

Dr. Tiffany Duncan DMD, PSC

  • Undergrad: B.S. Biology – Morehead State University
  • DMD: University of Louisville School of Dentistry (2000)
  • GPR Residency: University of Louisville Hospital
  • 25+ years in comprehensive family dentistry
  • Member: ADA, KDA

Outside the operatory, Dr. Duncan enjoys beach travel, binge‑worthy TV, and spoiling her Yorkies—Dolly & Diesel. She’s also a proud mom to Brooke & Emily, both pursuing biomedical science degrees at the University of Pikeville.

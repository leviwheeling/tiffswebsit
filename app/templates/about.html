Our Story

Duncan Family Dentistry has been proudly serving Belfry KY, South Williamson KY, Williamson WV, and surrounding Eastern Kentucky & Southern West Virginia communities for more than 25 years.

Founded by Dr. Tiffany Duncan DMD, our mission is simple: deliver state-of-the-art dental care with true hometown warmth.

We invest in the latest technology—from Digital Panoramic X-rays to Rotary Endodontics machines—so every visit is efficient, comfortable, and precise. Above all, we treat each patient like family, celebrating milestones and brightening smiles across generations.

Dental Medical Assistant

Full-Time

Position Overview

We are seeking a dedicated Dental Medical Assistant to join our team in both front and back office operations. This is a full-time position offering the opportunity to work in a family-oriented dental practice serving the Eastern Kentucky and Southern West Virginia communities.

Responsibilities

  • Cleaning and sterilizing dental instruments
  • Taking blood pressures and temperatures
  • Escorting patients to treatment rooms
  • Preparing and cleaning treatment rooms
  • Scheduling appointments and managing patient flow
  • Working with insurance companies and processing claims
  • Performing various front office administrative tasks
  • Assisting with patient care as needed

Requirements

  • High school diploma or equivalent (minimum education requirement)
  • Previous experience in the dental field preferred
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment
  • Detail-oriented and organized
  • Compassionate and patient-focused approach

What We Offer

  • Competitive compensation
  • Professional development opportunities
  • Family-friendly work environment
  • Supportive team atmosphere
